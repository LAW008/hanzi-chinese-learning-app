# Vocabulary data sources

The original 104 Hanzi entries remain in `vocabulary.js`. The expanded vocabulary in `vocabulary-extra.js` includes:

- Entries adapted from [complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary), Copyright (c) 2026 Yanis Zafirópulos, under the MIT License. The source data is filtered and normalized for this app; example sentences are generated for Hanzi.
- Entries adapted from [CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict), published by MDBG, under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The dictionary entries are filtered, numeric pinyin is converted to tone marks, and app example sentences are added.

The CC-CEDICT-derived portion of `vocabulary-extra.js` is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. If you reuse or redistribute that portion, retain this attribution and the license link, note your changes, and share adaptations under the same license.
